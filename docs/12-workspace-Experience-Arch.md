## 2. Core Experience Invariants

- **Intelligence before data:** Observations are contextualized before technical details are exposed.
- **Progressive disclosure:** Summary → Meaning → Change → Why → Technical Detail → Raw Evidence.
- **Silence is valuable:** If nothing changed, Nebula communicates stability without artificial filler.
- **Calm operational home:** Restrained aesthetics without aggressive alerts or dashboard clutter.
